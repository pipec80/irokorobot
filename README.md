# Iroko

> A local-first cognitive companion, built to be understandable and replaceable.

## Meet Iroko

[Iroko](docs/product/iroko-profile.md) is the product identity for a reusable, local-first cognitive brain. OMNiBot 2000 is its future experimental embodiment, not the product's current operating requirement.

## What exists today

**Implemented:** the versioned [current state](docs/architecture/current-state.md) records the capabilities and evidence available in this repository today.

## Principles that do not change

Iroko is local-first, explicit, and small enough to understand and replace. Its architecture keeps cognition, memory, policy, and any future embodiment separate; see the [architecture index](docs/architecture/README.md).

## Current boundaries

Physical-hardware guidance, autonomous action, and operational cloud escalation are not implemented. Local providers are the primary path; any cloud escalation is an authorized architectural boundary, not an automatic fallback.
